{{ lab.lab_name }} laboratory workstation.

What this software does

  • Registration: register the patient, select tests and profiles, apply a discount, take Cash or UPI (or leave it on Credit), and print the bill.
  • Master: create tests with species normal range, unit, interpretation, and rate; group tests into profiles; keep referring doctors and hospitals.
  • Reports: print a patient report by registration number, and collection reports by day, month, year, doctor, or hospital.
  • MIS: cash collection by Cash and UPI, and business by species.

Data is kept in a local database on this computer. Back up the database file regularly.
